Community Guidelines

IGY connects neighbors who want to help each other. These guidelines keep our community safe, respectful, and trustworthy.

Be Respectful

  • Treat everyone with kindness regardless of race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, or other characteristics.
  • No harassment, intimidation, or behavior that makes others feel unsafe.
  • Respect boundaries — no unwanted advances, persistent unwanted contact, or inappropriate communication.
  • Keep interactions related to help requests — don't contact users for unrelated purposes.

Appropriate Requests

  • Requests should be for genuine, practical help — things like picking up medication, a ride to a procedure, or a check-in during illness.
  • Be truthful about the nature and urgency of your requests.
  • IGY is not for emergencies — for medical, mental health, or law enforcement situations, contact the appropriate professionals.
  • No commercial activity, business promotion, or soliciting money, gifts, or favors in exchange for help.

Examples of requests that are not appropriate:

  • Ongoing or recurring tasks like "walk my dog every week" — IGY is for one-time help, not regular services.
  • Food or grocery delivery as a convenience — delivery apps are better suited for that.
  • Anything that could be handled by a paid professional service.

Safety

  • Don't put yourself or others in dangerous, isolated, or vulnerable situations.
  • Never share another user's contact information, photos, videos, or recordings without their explicit consent.
  • Don't create fake accounts or misrepresent your age, identity, location, or intentions.
  • Use your best judgment when meeting someone — trust your instincts.
  • For extra security, we recommend letting a friend or family member know where you're going, who you're meeting (such as the helper's name), or sharing your location.

Give Back to the Community

  • IGY runs on reciprocity — for every 3 requests you make, give back at least once.
  • Giving back can mean helping someone with their request, or contributing something uplifting like a joke, recipe, or encouraging message.
  • A healthy community depends on everyone both giving and receiving.

Violations

  • Breaking these guidelines may result in a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from IGY.
  • If you see behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it through the app.
  • For questions or concerns, contact us at support.igyapp@gmail.com.
